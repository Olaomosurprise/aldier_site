Education

Universities that run well, and graduates ready for work. A university runs housing, transport, catering, security, health and finance for thousands of people, all in service of teaching and research. We help universities and education systems strengthen their finances, operations and student experience, and prepare graduates for the labour market.

Our perspective

Students judge a university by its hostels, its portals, its queues and the jobs that follow graduation. The academic mission depends on everything around it.

How we help clients

Questions we help leaders answer

Most draw on more than one of our practices.

How do we fund growth without raising fees beyond reach?

Financial strategy, auxiliary enterprise and partnerships that diversify income responsibly.

How do we modernise the student journey?

Admissions, records, payments and learning platforms redesigned around students and staff.

How do we prepare graduates for work?

Employer partnerships, work-based learning and career services measured by outcomes.

How do we protect research and student data?

Security and data protection suited to open campuses and international collaboration.

How do we compete for international students and partners?

Positioning, identity and the experience prospective students meet online and on arrival.

How do we govern a multi-campus institution?

Operating models, decision rights and shared services for growing systems.
