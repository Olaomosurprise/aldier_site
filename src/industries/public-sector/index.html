Industry

Public Sector and Government

Public services that deliver for citizens, and reforms that last. Governments are judged by what citizens experience: the licence issued on time, the clinic that is open, the payment that arrives. We help ministries, agencies and regulators design reforms that can be delivered, and build the institutions that sustain them through changes of leadership.

Our perspective

Most of the work of reform happens after the announcement: in procurement, systems, people and the persistence to see it through. That is where we spend most of our time.

How we help clients

Questions we help leaders answer

Most draw on more than one of our practices.

How do we turn a manifesto into a delivery plan?

Priorities translated into programmes with owners, budgets and measures that a delivery unit can track week by week.

Which services should go digital first, and how?

Services chosen by volume and pain, redesigned around citizens, and built on infrastructure the state owns.

How do we buy technology without being captured by it?

Procurement strategies, specifications and contracts that keep the state in control of its systems and data.

How do we protect citizens' data?

Data protection programmes and security readiness for agencies that hold sensitive records.

How do we explain a difficult reform?

Public engagement and communication that prepares citizens for change before it reaches them.

How do we make reform outlast its sponsors?

Institutions, rules and capabilities designed to continue after the people who launched them have moved on.

Building delivery units

We help governments establish small, senior delivery units that track commitments against evidence, escalate problems early and publish progress that citizens can check.
