How we help clients

Questions we help leaders answer

Most draw on more than one of our practices.

Where should we focus our resources?

Portfolio strategy grounded in evidence of need, cost and likely impact.

How do we design a programme that scales?

Delivery models, partnerships and operating plans built for the second phase as well as the pilot.

How do we measure what we achieve?

Results frameworks, data systems and evaluations that boards and funders trust.

How do we strengthen our own organisation?

Governance, leadership, systems and safeguarding for organisations growing quickly.

Our perspective

Funders increasingly ask what changed, for whom and at what cost. Programmes designed to answer those questions from the start deliver more, and attract more support.
