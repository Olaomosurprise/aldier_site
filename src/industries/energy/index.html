How we help clients

Questions we help leaders answer

Most draw on more than one of our practices.

How do we improve operational and commercial performance?

Loss reduction, metering, billing and collection for utilities; productivity and cost for producers.

Which transition investments make sense for us?

Portfolio strategy for renewables, distributed generation and storage, tested against realistic demand and tariffs.

How do we deliver capital projects on time?

Project assurance, contracting strategy and governance for large investments.

How do we protect critical infrastructure?

Operational technology security, resilience and incident readiness for grids and plants.

How do we earn our licence to operate?

Community, regulatory and investor engagement grounded in transparent reporting.
