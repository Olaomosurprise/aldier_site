How we help clients

Questions we help leaders answer

Most draw on more than one of our practices.

Where should we invest along the value chain?

Value-chain economics, sourcing models and the partnerships that secure supply.

How do we reach smallholders at scale?

Aggregation, extension and payment models designed with farmers, tested in the field and costed before scale-up.

How do we use data and digital tools?

Platforms for traceability, credit scoring and market information that farmers and buyers will actually use.

How do we make a programme investable?

Business cases, risk sharing and reporting that give lenders and development funders confidence.

Our perspective

Most of the value lost in food systems disappears between the farm and the market: in storage, transport, finance and information. That is where we focus.
