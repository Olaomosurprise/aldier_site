Recruitment fraud warning

Fraudsters sometimes pose as well-known employers to obtain money or personal information from job seekers. Please read this before responding to any offer made in Aldier's name.

How Aldier recruits

  • We never ask candidates to pay a fee, deposit or charge of any kind at any stage of recruitment.
  • We never ask for bank details, identity documents or passwords before a formal offer has been made through our own process.
  • We never conduct interviews solely through messaging apps, and we never make an offer without at least one interview.
  • Opportunities at Aldier are published on our careers page.

Warning signs

  • A request for money for training, equipment, visas or processing.
  • An offer made without an interview, or one that seems too generous to be true.
  • Messages from personal email addresses or social media accounts claiming to represent Aldier.
  • Pressure to accept or pay quickly.

If you are contacted

Do not send money or personal information. Report the approach to us through the contact form, including any messages you received, and to your local police or fraud reporting service. Aldier accepts no liability for losses caused by people falsely claiming to act on its behalf.
