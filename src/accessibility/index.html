Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they rely on.

Last reviewed October 2026

Our standard

We design and build this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Pages can be used with a keyboard alone, with visible focus and a link to skip to the main content.
  • Text and controls meet contrast requirements, and the layout adapts to zoom and small screens.
  • Films are decorative, carry no essential information or sound, and can be paused. They stop when you ask your device to reduce motion.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Forms have visible labels and clear error messages.

Known limitations

Some scrolling effects are reduced when motion is minimised, and the arrangement of cards may differ slightly on very small screens. If anything prevents you from using the site, we want to know.

Tell us

Please describe the problem through the contact form, including the page and the technology you were using. We aim to reply within five working days.
