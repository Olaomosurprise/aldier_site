Practices / Communications and Public Affairs

05 · Explain

Trust with the people who decide your future.

Regulators, investors, staff, partners and the public each decide something about you. We help institutions give an honest, consistent account of themselves, and prepare for the days when that account is tested.

The problem we solve

Reputation is earned in practice and lost in public. Communication cannot rescue a decision that was wrong, but it can make sure a right decision is understood.

What we do

Six capabilities, one standard.

Each capability can be engaged on its own. Most clients find the work goes further when two or three are combined under one partner.

  1. Corporate reputation

    A clear account of what the institution stands for, built from what it does and tested with the people it must persuade.

  2. Crisis and issues

    Preparation, round-the-clock counsel and the statements that matter in the first hours of a crisis.

  3. Government relations and public affairs

    Policy analysis, engagement with officials and legislators, and coalitions built on shared interest, conducted openly and lawfully.

  4. Investor and financial communications

    Results, transactions and capital markets disclosure written to be read as well as filed.

  5. Internal and change communications

    Leaders who explain change before it happens, and staff who hear it from them first.

  6. Executive positioning

    Speeches, interviews and op-eds for leaders who have something worth saying.

Our principle

Our principle

We will help an institution explain a decision. We will not help it disguise one.

The standard every engagement in the practice is held to.
