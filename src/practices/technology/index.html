Capabilities

How we help clients

Clients engage these capabilities individually or in combination with other practices.

  1. Technology strategy and architecture

    A target architecture tied to the institution's priorities, sequenced for its budget and people.

  2. Selection and procurement advisory

    Requirements, market testing, evaluation and contracts that protect the buyer.

  3. Delivery assurance

    Independent oversight of major programmes, with early warning and clear reporting.

  4. Data and analytics

    Governance, platforms and reporting that leaders use to decide.

  5. Applied AI

    Use cases selected for value and risk, governed from the outset and measured in operation.

  6. Cloud and infrastructure

    Hosting, resilience and cost decisions based on evidence, with exit routes designed in.

Our perspective

The fate of a large technology programme is usually settled before any code is written: in its requirements, its contract and its timetable. That is where we concentrate our effort.

Independence

Independent of every vendor
