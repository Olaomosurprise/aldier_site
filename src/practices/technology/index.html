02 · Build

Systems that work, and data you can trust.

We sit on the client's side of the table: deciding what to build or buy, contracting for it, overseeing delivery and staying through the first months of live operation, when most programmes quietly fail.

The problem we solve

Most technology programmes fail before a line of code is written: in a requirement nobody tested, a contract that rewards the wrong thing, or a launch date chosen for a speech.

What we do

Six capabilities, one standard.

Each capability can be engaged on its own. Most clients find the work goes further when two or three are combined under one partner.

  1. Technology strategy and architecture

    A target architecture tied to what the institution must do, with a sequence it can afford and staff.

  2. Selection and procurement advisory

    Requirements, market testing, evaluation and contract terms written to protect the buyer.

  3. Delivery assurance

    Independent oversight of programmes in flight: early warning, honest status and the authority to call a halt.

  4. Data and analytics

    Data governance, platforms and the reporting leaders actually use to decide, with definitions everyone shares.

  5. Applied AI

    Use cases chosen for value and risk, governed from the start, and measured against the work they replace.

  6. Cloud and infrastructure

    Hosting, resilience and cost decisions taken on evidence, with an exit route designed in.
