04 · Design

Brands, products and places people choose to use.

Design is how an institution's decisions reach people: the service they queue for, the screen they tap, the building they enter, the film that tells them why it matters. The practice makes all of it, in Aldier Studio.

The problem we solve

People judge an institution by the last service it gave them. The strategy may be sound; if the form is confusing, the queue is long and the app fails, the strategy is invisible.

What we do

Six capabilities, one standard.

Each capability can be engaged on its own. Most clients find the work goes further when two or three are combined under one partner.

  1. Brand and identity

    Name, voice, visual identity and the guidelines that keep them consistent across every touchpoint.

  2. Service design

    Journeys mapped from observation, redesigned around the people who use them, and specified so they can be built.

  3. Digital products

    Websites, applications and platforms designed and built to accessibility standards, and measured once live.

  4. Film and motion

    Documentary, campaign and explainer films that carry an institution's story further than any report.

  5. Spatial and environmental design

    Branches, campuses, offices and public spaces planned around the service they house.

  6. Research and testing

    Interviews, observation and usability testing that replace opinion with evidence.
