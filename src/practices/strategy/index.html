Practices / Strategy and Transformation

01 · Decide

Decide what matters, and make it happen.

Strategy is only as good as the institution's ability to carry it out. We set direction with leadership teams, then stay to build the office, the measures and the habits that turn a plan into results.

The problem we solve

Leaders rarely lack a plan. What they lack is a plan the organisation can execute, a way of knowing whether it is working, and the discipline to stop what is not.

What we do

Six capabilities, one standard.

Each capability can be engaged on its own. Most clients find the work goes further when two or three are combined under one partner.

  1. Corporate and public strategy

    Where to compete or serve, what to stop, and the few choices that decide the rest. Written for the board and readable by everyone who must act on it.

  2. Operating model and organisation

    Structures, decision rights and governance that let people act without waiting for permission they should not need.

  3. Mergers, partnerships and restructuring

    Diligence, integration planning and separation, with the first hundred days designed before the announcement.

  4. Transformation office

    The engine room of change: a small team that tracks every commitment, surfaces problems early and keeps leadership honest about progress.

  5. Performance and cost

    Costs reduced where they do not serve the mission, and capacity redirected to where it does.

  6. Growth and new ventures

    New services, markets and businesses tested quickly and cheaply before they are funded properly.

How an engagement runs

Four stages, one team throughout.

The people who diagnose the problem stay to deliver the answer. Nobody hands a plan to strangers and leaves.

  1. Diagnose

    Six to eight weeks to establish the facts: the economics, the constraints, the people and the history of what has been tried before.

  2. Decide

    A small number of choices, set out with their costs and risks, for leadership to take and own. We recommend; you decide.

  3. Deliver

    A transformation office that tracks each commitment against named owners and dates, and reports progress without varnish.

  4. Hand over

    Capabilities transferred to your people, with the measures, routines and documentation to carry on without us.

Where the practice joins others

The practice works most often with these two. One partner leads, so the joins between them are nobody's blind spot.
