Standards

Editorial standards

Every piece names its authors and its reviewer. Every figure carries its source. Corrections are published openly, with the date and the reason, at the foot of the piece concerned.

  1. Signed

    Every argument carries its authors' names.

  2. Sourced

    Data and quotations traceable to their origin.

  3. Reviewed

    Read by an editor and a reviewer outside the author's team.
