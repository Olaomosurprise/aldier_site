Aldier, the multidisciplinary advisory firm, serves as Founding Partner to GENIVY, an international guild of students.

GENIVY is being developed as an independent student-centred guild, with meaningful student enterprise responsibility and professional institutional oversight. Its proposed operating model would connect existing campus businesses, student-managed enterprises, paid work and community programmes. Employer-recognised evidence and qualifications require appropriate verification and authorisation.

Aldier designed GENIVY's economic model, its constitution and governance, its identity and its digital platform, drawing on all five of the firm's practices. GENIVY's proposed constitutional architecture addresses asset protection, trustee independence, conflicts of interest and the limits of founder influence; its final legal provisions require formal approval.

Proposed Founding Partner arrangements describe Aldier's advisory role, potential nomination rights and documented handover responsibilities, subject to legal review and approval. A nonprofit guild has no equity shareholders; Aldier's position must preserve the institution's independence. The operating model contemplates a staged, documented handover to GENIVY's own management, governed by agreed milestones and independent oversight.

About Aldier

Aldier is a multidisciplinary advisory firm working across strategy and transformation, technology and data, cybersecurity and digital risk, design and experience, and communications and public affairs.

About GENIVY

GENIVY is a proposed independent student guild that approaches each campus as a functioning economy. Its long-term vision connects student-managed enterprises, existing independent businesses, programmes and institutional infrastructure across campuses and countries. More information is available at GENIVY's website.
