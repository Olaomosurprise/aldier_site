Aldier's founding work on GENIVY

Aldier has developed the institutional model and digital presence for GENIVY, an initiative building infrastructure for the campus economy.

The model connects student-managed enterprises, independent businesses, paid academic roles, commercial premises and community programmes. Universities can commission services individually or explore a broader campus partnership. Students take responsibility for enterprise teams and budgets within professional institutional oversight.

Aldier's work covers the economic model, governance architecture, identity and digital platform. The constitutional draft addresses asset stewardship, trustee independence, conflicts of interest and founder succession.

The institution remains in its founding phase. GENIVY’s prospective members would elect trustees under its constitution. Appointments and campus agreements remain subject to adoption. The founding partner would have no permanent office or veto. A documented handover assigns systems, records and responsibilities to GENIVY's management.

About Aldier

Aldier is a multidisciplinary advisory firm working across strategy and transformation, technology and data, cybersecurity and digital risk, design and experience, and communications and public affairs.

About GENIVY

Every campus is a micro-economy of work, culture and ideas. GENIVY is an independent global institution that partners with tertiary institutions to give that economy structure and scale, connecting students, affiliated businesses, faculties and existing campus systems.

GENIVY describes its purpose in these terms. “Through purpose-built estates and subsidised spaces, student-managed enterprises, leadership programmes and paid roles in research, business and entrepreneurship, we build the institutions a campus economy runs on. Together, we strengthen tertiary institutions and enrich the civic, cultural and economic life of their campuses, leaving a legacy of impact for generations to come.”
