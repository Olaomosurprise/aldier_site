Aldier, the multidisciplinary advisory firm, serves as Founding Partner to GENIVY, an international guild of students.

GENIVY is owned by its student members. It organises the work already present on university campuses, among them laundry, printing, tutoring and design, pays students to run it, and certifies what they achieve through councils of practitioners in each sector.

Aldier designed GENIVY's economic model, its constitution and governance, its identity and its digital platform, drawing on all five of the firm's practices. GENIVY's constitution places five protections beyond the reach of any vote, including a bar on outside shareholders and an asset lock, and establishes an independent Board of Trustees.

As Founding Partner, Aldier nominates the Vice-Chair of GENIVY's Board of Trustees and the Vice-Chair of its Advancement Council, and gives its advice, time and expertise without charge under a published Founding Partner Commitment. It holds no shares in the guild. Each function Aldier helps to build passes to GENIVY's own team under a staged handover, with every transfer published.

About Aldier

Aldier is a multidisciplinary advisory firm working across strategy and transformation, technology and data, cybersecurity and digital risk, design and experience, and communications and public affairs.

About GENIVY

GENIVY is an international guild owned by its student members. On each university campus it builds the institutions of a working economy, and it is organised as a federation of national guilds. More information is available at GENIVY's website.
