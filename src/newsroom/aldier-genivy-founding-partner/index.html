Aldier's founding work on GENIVY

October 2026 · Updated 20 November 2026

Aldier has developed the institutional model and digital presence for GENIVY, an independent institution developing economic and civic infrastructure for tertiary education.

The model connects student-managed enterprises, independent businesses, paid academic roles, commercial premises and community programmes. Universities can commission services individually or explore a broader campus partnership. Students take responsibility for enterprise teams and budgets within professional institutional oversight.

Aldier's work covers the economic model, governance architecture, identity and digital platform. The constitutional draft addresses asset stewardship, trustee independence, conflicts of interest and founder succession.

The institution remains in its founding phase. GENIVY’s prospective members would elect trustees under its constitution. Appointments and institutional partnership agreements remain subject to adoption. The founding partner would have no permanent office or veto. A documented handover assigns systems, records and responsibilities to GENIVY's management.

About Aldier

About GENIVY

GENIVY is an independent global institution developing economic and civic infrastructure for tertiary education. We work with universities, polytechnics and colleges to connect students, faculties and affiliated businesses through existing institutional systems.

GENIVY describes its purpose in these terms. “Working with tertiary institutions, we develop purpose-built estates and subsidised spaces, support student-managed enterprises and leadership programmes, and create paid roles in research, business and entrepreneurship. Together, we strengthen tertiary institutions and enrich the civic, cultural and economic life of their communities, leaving a legacy of impact for generations to come.”

More information is available at GENIVY's website.
