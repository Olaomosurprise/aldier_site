Aldier's founding work on GENIVY

October 2026 · Updated 4 November 2026
Media enquiriesContact the media team
RelatedCase study

Aldier has developed the institutional model and digital presence for GENIVY, an initiative building infrastructure for the campus economy.

The model connects student-managed enterprises, independent businesses, paid academic roles, commercial premises and community programmes. Universities can commission services individually or explore a broader campus partnership. Students take responsibility for enterprise teams and budgets within professional institutional oversight.

Aldier's work covers the economic model, governance architecture, identity and digital platform. The constitutional draft addresses asset stewardship, trustee independence, conflicts of interest and founder succession.

The institution remains in its founding phase. Appointments, operating agreements and Aldier's formal Founding Partner rights require adoption through the relevant legal instruments. A documented handover assigns systems, records and responsibilities to GENIVY's management.

About Aldier

Aldier is a multidisciplinary advisory firm working across strategy and transformation, technology and data, cybersecurity and digital risk, design and experience, and communications and public affairs.

About GENIVY

GENIVY is building infrastructure for the campus economy through enterprise, employment, shared facilities and community investment. Its Guild connects students to those opportunities and to one another. More information is available at GENIVY's website.
