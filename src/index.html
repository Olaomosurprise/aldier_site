    Our impact in action

    GENIVY

    GENIVY is the international guild of students that Aldier helped found. Owned by its members, it organises the work already present on university campuses, pays students to run it and certifies what they achieve. Aldier designed its economic model, constitution, identity and digital platform, and provides strategy, design and technology as its Founding Partner.
