The Board Programme

For the people who answer last.

Directors are accountable for risks that few of them were trained to oversee: cyber attacks, artificial intelligence, data protection and public reputation. The Board Programme gives boards the questions, the practice and the confidence to govern them.

Formats

Four formats, held in private.

Every session is held under the Chatham House Rule, delivered by Aldier partners, and tailored to the board in the room.

90 minutes

Board briefing

A focused session on one risk, with the questions a board should ask management this quarter.

Half day

Incident rehearsal

A live cyber or reputational crisis worked in real time, with directors taking the decisions that would fall to them.

Two sessions

Director induction

Technology, data and digital risk for newly appointed directors and audit committee members.

Annual

Board review

An independent look at how the board oversees technology and risk, with a written report to the chair.

Topics

The risks on every board agenda.

  • Cyber risk and incident readiness

  • Artificial intelligence: opportunity, risk and governance

  • Data protection and privacy obligations

  • Reputation, crisis and stakeholder trust

  • Overseeing large technology programmes
