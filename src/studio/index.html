Why a studio inside a consultancy

Most advice ends as a document handed to someone else to build. Much of its value is lost in translation. The Studio closes that gap by putting the makers in the room where the decisions are taken.

How the Studio works

From evidence to launch, and after.

Every Studio project follows the same four steps, whatever is being made.

  1. Observe

    Research with the people who will use the thing: interviews, observation, analytics and the complaints file.

  2. Prototype

    Ideas made tangible early, in paper, code, film or foam board, and tested before money is committed.

  3. Make

    Production to a written standard, with accessibility, security and brand checks built into the work.

  4. Measure

    After launch, the Studio returns to see whether people use it as intended, and fixes what they do not.
