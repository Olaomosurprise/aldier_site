Recruitment

How we hire

Every candidate follows the same process, assessed against the same criteria.

  1. Application

    Your CV or portfolio, and a short note on the work you want to do.

  2. Assessment

    A written exercise or portfolio review suited to your discipline.

  3. Interviews

    Conversations with colleagues and a partner, including a case drawn from real client problems.

  4. Offer

    A decision and feedback, whatever the outcome.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a business degree?

No. We hire from every discipline our practices need, including engineering, design, architecture, law, the humanities and the sciences.

Can I apply to more than one practice?

Yes. Tell us which practices interest you; many of our people work across two or more.

Will I ever be asked to pay to apply?

Never. Aldier does not charge candidates at any stage. Read our recruitment fraud warning.
