How we work

Our commitments to clients

Each commitment is written into our engagement letters.

  1. One accountable partner

    A named partner answers for the whole engagement, from the first meeting to the handover.

  2. One team across the practices

    Specialists from every practice the problem needs, named at the start and changed only with the client's agreement.

  3. Independent review

    Every deliverable is checked by someone outside the team, and carries the names of the people who made and reviewed it.

  4. Independent assurance

    Systems Aldier builds are tested and certified by independent parties.

  5. Objective advice

    Our recommendations stand on evidence and are independent of any further Aldier engagement. Every figure we present is traceable to its source.

Our standards

Independence

Aldier accepts no commissions, referral fees or reseller margins from technology vendors, media owners or any other supplier we may recommend. Before each engagement we check for conflicts of interest and disclose any we find. Where a conflict cannot be managed, we decline the work.
