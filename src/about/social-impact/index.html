Our impact in action

GENIVY

GENIVY is the international guild of students for which Aldier serves as Founding Partner. Owned by its members, it organises the work already present on university campuses, pays students to run it and certifies what they achieve, so that students leave with income, experience and a record employers can trust.

As Founding Partner, Aldier gives strategy, governance, design and technology without charge, and its nominees serve as Vice-Chairs of GENIVY's Board of Trustees and Advancement Council. Each function Aldier helps to build passes to GENIVY's own team under a staged handover.
