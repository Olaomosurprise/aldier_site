Research standards

Independent research

  • Methods published first. Every study states its question, sources and method before results are released.
  • Reviewed outside the team. Each publication is read by reviewers who did not take part in the research.
  • No sponsor edits. Where research is funded by a partner, the funder sees findings at the same time as the public and cannot change them.
  • Clients unnamed without consent. Engagement knowledge informs the research; client identities never appear without written agreement.
