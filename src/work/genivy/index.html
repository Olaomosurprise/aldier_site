GENIVY: designing an institution for the campus economy

GENIVY is building infrastructure for the campus economy. Its model connects student-run ventures, independent businesses, paid academic roles, commercial premises and community funding. Aldier developed the economic proposition, governance architecture, identity and digital presence that bring those functions together.

The opportunity

A university sustains demand for goods, services, work and shared space across successive generations of students. Independent businesses meet much of that demand. GENIVY's design connects them with student enterprise, paid assignments and university services, while developing the facilities and funding that support campus life.

The solution

Designing the institution end to end

The institutional design draws on strategy, governance, technology, identity and communications, with operating decisions documented for GENIVY's future leadership.

  1. Economic model

    A connected portfolio of student enterprises, merchant services, workforce coordination, paid academic roles, commercial premises and community programmes. Universities can commission individual services or a broader campus partnership. Property rights and operating responsibilities are agreed for each project.

  2. Strategy and governance

    A proposed constitution, independent trustee oversight, student-managed enterprises and a long-term international affiliation model, subject to legal and institutional approval.

  3. Standards and accountability

    Practitioner advice, service standards and reporting schedules connect operational responsibility to student pay, customer experience and programme outcomes.

  4. Technology and data

    Membership, verified experience and service records are designed around student control of disclosure. Banking, payments and access integration have separate providers and mandates.

  5. Data protection and safeguarding

    Draft policies and controls for member data, safeguarding, funds and operational accountability, to be adopted and implemented through the appropriate governing bodies.

  6. Identity, website and relations

    The name, identity and institutional website, designed and built in Aldier Studio, together with the case to patrons, universities and partners.

Project status

Institutional design and implementation

The founding model, institutional documents and public website are developed. The documents are working instruments for adoption and implementation. Incorporation, appointments and campus agreements require confirmation before operations begin.

Aldier's continuing advisory role and any nomination rights will be governed by adopted legal instruments. The handover schedule assigns records, systems and responsibilities to GENIVY's management.
